<objective>
Build P4 — the L2 nowcaster diagnostics notebook. It surfaces the walk-forward's real,
already-persisted forecast-quality numbers (Brier, calibration, confusion) checked against a
stated plausibility band, and complements them with a fresh, cheap, non-walk-forward fit that
exposes the persistence-trap (transition-window vs steady-state accuracy) design section 5.1
warns about.

Purpose: the codebase's own multiclass Brier formula bounds to [0, 1], not the textbook [0, 2],
with a K=5 no-skill floor of 0.16 — and the observed 0.2087 sits ABOVE that floor. This is not
a defect Phase 6 fixes (no tuning); it is a finding Phase 6 must surface honestly, corroborating
audit item A8. P4 is where an operator sees this plainly, alongside the persistence-trap check
that a bare "90% accuracy" headline would otherwise hide.

Output: a new `platform/plotting/nowcaster.py` submodule (diagnostic fit orchestration, four plot
functions), its test file, and the committed `P4_nowcaster.ipynb`.
