<objective>
Build the monthly macro/long-history raw ingestion: FRED market series resampled to
month-end, plus multpl and macrotrends parsed to monthly frequency by reusing the
incumbent's low-level fetch/parse helpers without editing them.

Purpose: This is the heart of DATA-01 — the switch from a quarterly spine (~260 obs since
1950) to a monthly spine (~770 obs since 1962). The incumbent fetchers hardcode `.resample
("QE")`, so this plan writes thin monthly analogs that reuse the client/parse patterns but
target `"ME"` (RESEARCH Pitfall 1).

Output: `platform/ingestion/macro_monthly.py` and its mocked unit test.
</objective>
