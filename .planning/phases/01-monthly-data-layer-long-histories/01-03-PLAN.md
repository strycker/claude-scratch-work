<objective>
Implement ALFRED point-in-time vintage ingestion for the revision-heavy agency series
(D-06: GDP, CPI, UNRATE, INDPRO, PAYEMS) and document the pre-vintage-era fallback.

Purpose: Publication-lag shift alone fixes *timing* look-ahead but not *revision* look-
ahead — using a final revised GDP figure is still cheating even when correctly dated
(RESEARCH Pitfall 4). ALFRED vintages give the value that was actually published as of
each date. This plan produces the bulk vintage fetch and the point-in-time reconstruction
the transforms layer (Plan 07) applies.

Output: `platform/ingestion/alfred.py`, its mocked unit test, and `docs/vintage_alignment.md`.
</objective>
