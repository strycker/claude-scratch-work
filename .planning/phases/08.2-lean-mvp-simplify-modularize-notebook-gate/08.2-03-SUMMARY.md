## What was built

**Task 1 (tracer): RED 9b73a99, GREEN bd6666a.**
- `monitor.evaluate_tripwire(cfg, cm, *, run_date)`. It loads `daily_raw["SPY"]` and `fred_daily_raw[["fred_daaa","fred_dbaa"]]` separately; a missing checkpoint or column makes the affected signals UNAVAILABLE and never raises. NaNs are dropped per series and DAAA/DBAA are aligned on common days. `triggered` comes from the existing bool functions on the cleaned inputs. `value`/`threshold` come from the new NaN-returning helpers. - `weekly.build_weekly_page(cfg, cm, *, output_dir)`: inputs, then the tripwire on the same `cm` at `_run_date()`, then assemble and write. `main` delegates to it.
- `_tripwire_section`: renders `## Crash Tripwire (advisory — changes no weight)` before the trades heading. Bullets show RED/GREEN, "STALE" or "STALE (would be RED)", or UNAVAILABLE with the rebuild command. "Escalation: none" appears only when all three signals are current and green; otherwise, with no red, it reads "Escalation: UNKNOWN (k of 3 signals current — not green)".
