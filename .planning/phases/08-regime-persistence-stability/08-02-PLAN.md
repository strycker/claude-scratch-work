<objective>
Diagnose Track A before anything changes it: measure how much of classifier #1's 41.91% filtered
churn is an artefact of reading the **terminal month** of a dynamic-programming decode.

Purpose: ROADMAP criterion 3. The DP minimises `sum_t d[t, s_t] + lambda * sum_t 1[s_t != s_{t-1}]`
(`labeling/jump_model.py:43-45`). The terminal month is the only month with no right-hand
neighbour, so deviating there costs **lambda once** where an interior deviation costs **2*lambda**
(jump in and jump out). The filtered labeling reads exactly that month, every step
(`joint_driver.py:502`, `state_1 = states_1.iloc[-1]`). The *existence* of that asymmetry follows
from the objective; its *magnitude* is unmeasured, and this is the measurement.

The second, additive explanation is lambda relative to the fitted feature dimension: classifier #1
runs at lambda/d = 10.0/10 = **1.0** and churns 41.91%; classifier #2 runs at 16.0/8 = **2.0** and
churns **4.09%** through the identical code path. Measuring churn as a function of k separates the
two: if churn falls sharply with k, a large share of the 41.91% is an **edge artefact of reading
`iloc[-1]`**; if churn is flat in k, the edge story is refuted and lambda/d is the whole story.

Output: a `(step, k, classifier)` label matrix on disk, a churn-vs-k table, and a record that
states the reading and does not invent a threshold.

**Scope fence, restated because it is the expensive one.** The zero-trial diagnostic is IN scope.
A **lambda sweep is NOT.** A sweep costs one registry trial per value against 42 of a 44 ceiling
(ADR-0002) and needs its own ruling (CONTEXT amendment, Q-7 resolution). This plan fits the
labeler at its **pinned** (K, lambda) only — K=6/lambda=10.0 for classifier #1, K=5/lambda=16.0
for classifier #2 — and therefore evaluates no configuration and registers nothing. If the
churn-vs-k curve suggests a different lambda would help, that is a **finding to record**, not a
change to make here.

**This plan changes no production code.** It adds a script and a test. `driver.py`,
`joint_driver.py` and everything under `labeling/` are read-only to it. That is deliberate: the
diagnostic must measure the system as it stands on this branch, and a diagnostic that modifies the
thing it measures cannot be compared against the tracked curves.
</objective>
