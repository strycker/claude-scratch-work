<objective>
Pin validation gap G6: non-joint consumers of `vol_targeted_tilt` receive the **unpooled**
per-regime estimate.

Purpose: ROADMAP criterion 8. The wave-2 Nyquist audit of Phase 7 identified G6 and could not
write it because `driver.py` was outside its write scope. The seam is located and the asymmetry is
real:

- `backtest/driver.py:490` builds `stats = returns_by_regime_stats(dev_asset_returns.loc[train_index], states)`
  and `:497` passes it straight into `vol_targeted_tilt(regime_probs, stats, ...)`. **Raw. Unpooled.**
- `report/weekly.py:245-249` loads `returns_by_regime` from the checkpoint and passes it straight
  into `vol_targeted_tilt`. **Also unpooled.**
- `allocation/joint_tilt.py:319` applies `pool_low_n_regime_sharpe` before tilting, and says so in
  its own comment at `:357`: *"condition (iv)'s partial pooling, which `vol_targeted_tilt` does
  not."*

Output: one test file pinning all three, and a short record of what the pin covers.

**The pin is of the NON-compliance, and that is the whole design.** ADR-0001 condition (iv)
requires partial pooling of sub-floor regimes' Sharpes. Two of three consumers do not do it.
Writing this test as *"the driver's tilt receives a condition-(iv)-compliant frame"* would produce
a test that **can only pass** once someone makes it compliant, and that fails today for the right
reason but tells a future reader nothing about what the code actually does. Pinning the
non-compliance means the day someone fixes it, this test goes red and they must consciously move
it — which is exactly the visibility an open audit item is supposed to buy. This project has six
recorded instances of the check that can only confirm; G6 written the other way round would be the
seventh.

**And the both-halves rule, because one half is not enough.** `pool_low_n_regime_sharpe` is a
**strict no-op** for a labeling whose regimes all sit at or above the occupancy floor — its own
docstring says so. So "the observed frame equals the unpooled table" is satisfied by a pooled
implementation too, whenever the fixture has no sub-floor regime. Every arm therefore uses a
fixture with at least one sub-floor regime and asserts **both** halves: equal to unpooled **and**
different from pooled, with the differing cell named in the assertion message. A one-sided
assertion here is the same defect in a new costume.

**Out of scope, stated so it is not read as an omission.** ADR-0001 condition (iv)'s **covariance
clause** is explicitly NOT in scope for this phase (`08-CONTEXT.md` scope fences): no per-regime
covariance exists at L4-01 and it falls to L3 per design §6.2. G6 pins the **Sharpe** pooling seam
only. This plan also makes no attempt to *fix* the non-compliance — fixing it changes allocation
weights, which changes criterion 7, and that is not a change this phase authorises.
</objective>
