<objective>
Persist the per-step probability matrix, and split criterion 2's churn into the two series that
actually exist — Track A (an L1 quantity) and Track B (an L2 quantity, never measured anywhere).

Purpose: ROADMAP criterion 0 is the hard prerequisite for criteria 1, 2 and 4. `driver.py:530-532`
accumulates `per_step_metrics["proba"]` and `joint_driver.py:508-510` accumulates it per
classifier; `joint_driver.py:568-569` returns both buckets; `scripts/run_joint_lift.py:251-260`
persists **only the equity curves**. Nothing about the nowcaster's filtered path is measurable
until that changes. This plan is the phase's tracer: one thin path from the driver's in-memory
accumulator, through a persisted artifact, to a two-series churn record, end to end, with a real
runnable check at the far end.

Output: a new `evaluation/churn.py`, four probability parquets, and regenerated diagnostics JSONs
in which Track A and Track B appear as separate, separately-denominated rows.

**The single fact this plan exists to make un-fudgeable.** The recorded 41.91% is
`state_1` churn — the **jump model's** terminal-month label
(`joint_driver.py:502`, `state_1 = states_1.iloc[-1]`). Under the decision-bearing
`ROUTING_L1_ONLY`, `probs_1 = _last_state_one_hot(states_1)` (`joint_driver.py:431`, one-hot
constructed at `:251`) and `_refit_l2` is never called. A §5.1-style change to L2 therefore
**cannot move Track A**, and a criterion that re-measures Track A after an L2 fix reports
246 → 246 whether the fix worked perfectly or not at all. That is the sixth recorded instance of
this project's signature defect — a check that can only confirm — and it was caught in this
phase's own research. This plan's job is to make the two objects structurally impossible to
confuse: **two files, two functions, two rows, two denominators.**

**Verified at plan time, re-derived from the tracked artifacts on this branch:**
`state_1` changes **246** and `state_2` changes **24** over 587 adjacent pairs, identically under
both routings. `active_regime` is elementwise equal to `state_1` under `l1only` (0 degraded
steps); under `l2` it changes **462**, is `None` in **387** of 588 months, and **100** steps are
degraded. Every one of those numbers is reproducible from
`outputs/reports/platform/joint_lift/joint_lift_joint_{l1only,l2}.parquet`.

**Registry cost: zero.** Every run in this plan uses `--routing l2` (already `NO_REGISTRY`, the
observational leg per ADR-0002 decision (e)) or `--routing l1 --dry-run` (which forces
`NO_REGISTRY` via `decision_bearing = (routing == ROUTING_L1_ONLY) and not dry_run`). The ledger
is at **42 of a 44 ceiling** and the phase's one remaining decision-bearing run belongs to plan
08-10. Any run in this plan that appends a registry row is a defect, and Task 1 asserts the
count is unchanged.
</objective>
