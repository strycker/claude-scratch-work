## 3. The ruling

**Glenn selects option `b-promote-dsr`: the answer to A11 is YES — this project shall carry one
gate that fails on a bad-but-working model, and that gate is the deflated-Sharpe hurdle.**

### 3.1 The gate, stated as a checkable predicate

A decision-bearing criterion-7 leg **PASSES** the quality tier if and only if its own estimated
Sharpe ratio exceeds the expected maximum Sharpe of an equal number of skill-less trials:

```
observed_sharpe  >  expected_max_sharpe(total_trial_count(), sharpe_variance)
```

Equivalently, and identically in arithmetic, `deflated_sharpe_ratio(...) > 0.5` — because
`deflated_sharpe_ratio` is `norm.cdf` of a z whose numerator is exactly
`observed_sharpe − expected_max_sharpe(...)`. At `observed_sharpe` equal to the hurdle the DSR is
exactly **0.5**, which is why `format_dsr_verdict`'s existing `_VERDICT_HURDLE = 0.5` is the same
threshold and no new constant is invented by this decision.

At the recorded count of **42** trials and the placeholder variance **1.0**, the hurdle is

```
expected_max_sharpe(42, 1.0) = 2.208694        (2.2086935028832686)
```

The hurdle is **computed live, never a literal.** At 44 trials — the ADR-0002 ceiling — it rises to
2.2268911497604993. A trial spent anywhere in this project raises the bar its own results must
clear. That is the property that makes this gate worth having.

### 3.2 Why this one and not another

`expected_max_sharpe(n_trials, sharpe_variance)` is **arithmetic on the trial count** — Bailey &
López de Prado (2014) Eq. 5/6, an Extreme Value Theory result. It is **the only quality threshold
in this project that is *derived* rather than asserted by analogy.** The four plausibility bands
are all `[ASSUMED]`; this is not. It already exists, is already computed on every run, already
carries an unsoftened verdict string, and it gates on exactly the failure this project's honesty
framework was built to catch: a result indistinguishable from a skill-less discovery given the
search actually performed.

### 3.3 The three rejected answers, and why

- **`a-no-gate`** (answer NO; bands stay plausibility-only) — rejected. It is the cheapest answer
  and it preserves D-07 exactly, but it ships the platform with **no** gate that can fail on a bad
  model, and §2's instance is now on the record as the documented consequence. The reversal would
  have been real but modest: the item stops being open without anything behaving differently.
- **`c-promote-domain-bands`** (promote the existing advisory tier to governing) — rejected
  **decisively, and on measurement rather than on argument.** On the actual result
  (`wealth_delta` −0.123438, `dd_delta` +0.024084) **both domain flags are already recorded
  `False`.** Promoting them buys the *appearance* of a quality gate with none of the substance,
  while incurring the full weight of §0.1's `[ASSUMED]` objection. Tightening them toward the
  observed values to make them bite would convert a plausibility band into a quality band fitted to
  a result already seen — which is precisely what D-07 and ADR-0001's selection criterion refuse.
- **`d-preregister-new`** (a brand-new pre-registered threshold) — rejected. Calibrating one would
  spend from the **two-row headroom** that `08-09` needs, and a pre-registered number chosen from
  intuition is still an intuition; it is merely an honest one. There is no measurement in this
  project from which to derive such a threshold — which is exactly why the existing four are all
  `[ASSUMED]`.

### 3.4 The consequence, accepted knowingly and in advance

**Criterion 7's already-recorded ✅ MET (ROADMAP, 2026-09-21) becomes FAILED retroactively, on
BOTH legs** — at DSR **2.28151091802503 × 10⁻¹²** (`#1-alone`, observed Sharpe 0.9170725133308871)
and **1.4690427074211624 × 10⁻¹¹** (joint, observed Sharpe 0.914903185594245), each against the
`expected_max_sharpe(42, 1.0)` = **2.208694** hurdle. Neither leg's Sharpe is within a factor of
two of the bar.

This consequence is **accepted in advance, at the moment of the decision, not discovered at plan
08-10.** The measurement itself does not change and is not re-described: `wealth_delta` remains
−0.123438 nats over 588 steps and criterion 7 remains MET *as a measurement*. What changes is that
"measured, and reported honestly" is no longer sufficient for a **quality** verdict on a
decision-bearing leg. Criterion 7 is met as a measurement and **failed as a result**, and the
record now says both.

### 3.5 Registry arithmetic

**registry rows spent: 0.** Promotion of an already-computed quantity costs no new trials. Live
`total_trial_count()` stands at **42** against ADR-0002's ceiling of **44**. This decision leaves
**2 rows** of headroom, in full, for `08-09` Task 1's `b-bounded-turnover` option. `08-10`'s
closing reconciliation parses the phrase above from this document and from `08-A7.md` and asserts
the live registry equals 42 plus the two recorded spends.

number produced by this phase existed. Verified at the moment of writing: no

## 4. Whether the prior objection survives

**07-BANDS §8's `[ASSUMED]` objection is NOT answered and NOT overridden. It is *narrowed*, and
what remains of it is accepted as a cost, in writing.**

- **Narrowed, and the narrowing is real.** §8's objection is that promoting a band to a gate means
  gating on numbers *no measurement in this project derived*. The DSR hurdle is not such a number:
  `expected_max_sharpe(42, ·)`'s dependence on the trial count is derived arithmetic, and the trial
  count itself is a **live read of this project's own registry**. On the axis §8 actually objects
  to — provenance of the threshold — this gate is categorically different from the four bands.
- **What survives, stated without softening.** `sharpe_variance = 1.0` is
  `DEGENERATE_SHARPE_VARIANCE`, **a declared placeholder, not a measured quantity**, standing until
  **20 independent Sharpe-bearing trials** exist (`_MIN_USABLE_SHARPE_OBSERVATIONS`, raised 2 → 20
  by Glenn on 2026-09-21 after n=2 was measured to collapse the hurdle by 99.85%). So the
  **2.208694** figure rests on a declared assumption. **That is the same species of objection as
  §8's, and it is not waved away.**
- **Why it is nonetheless a weaker form of it.** The placeholder is (i) **conservative by
  construction** — 1.0 keeps the multiple-testing correction mechanically active, where 0.0 would
  silently switch it off entirely, the under-penalization T-07-05 names as this project's closest
  analog to a security defect; (ii) **documented in code, at its definition, as an assumption**;
  and (iii) carries a **stated path to replacement** with a specific, countable trigger. The four
  `[ASSUMED]` bands have none of these three properties. A conservative, documented assumption with
  a replacement trigger is not the same object as a round number chosen by analogy — but it is not
  a measurement either, and this record does not pretend otherwise.
- **Carried forward, not closed.** ADR-0002 open item 8 (*"`DEGENERATE_SHARPE_VARIANCE = 1.0`
  remains a declared assumption governing every DSR this project reports, until 20 independent
  Sharpe-bearing trials exist"*) remains open and is now **load-bearing on a gate** rather than on
  a report. That escalation is the cost of this decision and is recorded as such. When the
  twentieth independent Sharpe-bearing trial lands, the hurdle moves, and every verdict this gate
  has issued is re-dated.

## 5. What this now licenses and forbids

### 5.1 The gate's exact trigger

A **decision-bearing** leg (`decision_bearing: true`) of a criterion-7 measurement **FAILS the
quality tier** when

```
deflated_sharpe_ratio(observed_sharpe, total_trial_count(), sharpe_variance, skew, kurtosis, n_obs) <= 0.5
```

equivalently when `observed_sharpe <= expected_max_sharpe(total_trial_count(), sharpe_variance)`.
Both arguments to the hurdle are read **live** at measurement time; neither may be a literal.

### 5.2 What it licenses

- A criterion-7 verdict may now be framed as *"failed the quality tier"* — a sentence this project
  could not previously write about any result, only about a broken measurement.
- A future result whose Sharpe clears the live hurdle may be described as having **passed** a
  derived quality gate. No other verdict in this project may be described that way.

### 5.3 What it forbids

- **The four plausibility bands remain plausibility-only.** D-07 is untouched. A green
  `*_universal_ok` still means only *the measurement is possible*; a `*_domain_note` is still a
  recorded note that changes no verdict. Nothing in this decision promotes any `[ASSUMED]` band,
  and no future record may cite this document as having done so.
- **No band may be tightened toward an observed value.** Unchanged and restated.
- **The hurdle may not be written as a literal**, lowered, or computed at anything other than the
  live `total_trial_count()`. A hard-coded 2.208694 would freeze the bar while the search kept
  growing — the exact under-penalization this gate exists to prevent.
- **A failure is not waivable by a reviewer.** Reviewers still approve on the honesty and
  completeness of a record, never on whether the numbers look good (D-06). A failed quality tier
  is **reported**, not negotiated. It may be superseded only by a later ADR that states what
  changed, or **re-dated** when `sharpe_variance` stops being a placeholder — never by a reading
  of the same numbers.
- **No 2021+ holdout data may inform this gate, its threshold, or any waiver of it.**

### 5.4 Handoff — the code consequence is NOT executed here

This plan writes **no code in `src/`**. Any code change this decision implies — surfacing the
quality-tier verdict alongside the existing `*_universal_ok` / `*_domain_note` flags in
`joint_lift_table`, and honouring it in the criterion-7 record — is executed by **plan 08-10**,
under this decision, **before criterion 7 is re-measured**. `joint_driver.py` is owned by `08-01`
in this wave and by `08-10` in wave 5; editing its band constants from here would be a
cross-plan write. The dependency graph enforces the ordering: **08-10 depends on 08-05.**
