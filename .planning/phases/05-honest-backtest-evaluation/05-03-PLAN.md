<objective>
Build the evaluation primitives that turn an equity curve into the phase's reported numbers:
strategy KPIs (`kpis.py` — terminal log wealth, max drawdown + duration, CVaR(5%),
in-sample crisis capture ratios) and the EVAL-03 headline orchestration (`sojourn_lag.py` —
median sojourn ÷ detection lag). Both are pure functions over already-frozen building blocks:
`sojourn_lag.py` invents ZERO new math (it wires `occupancy_and_sojourns` +
`gap_lag.compute_detection_lag` into `gap_lag.sojourn_lag_ratio`), and the KPIs are ~30 lines
of auditable vectorized pandas the project already writes by hand elsewhere (no
empyrical/quantstats dependency).

Purpose: The sojourn/detection-lag ratio is the go/no-go number the report shows FIRST
(D-01a, §5.4). The crisis capture ratios are the crash-avoidance evidence — and are exactly
where a config typo could leak a holdout date, so the window bound is enforced here.
Output: `evaluation/kpis.py`, `evaluation/sojourn_lag.py`, and their two test files.
</objective>
