<objective>
Build the L3 asset-prediction analytics: returns-by-regime tables (L3-01) and per-asset EWMA
volatility forecasts (L3-02). Both are pure composition over already-persisted data — the
`monthly_raw` checkpoint carries every asset's month-end level and `regime_labels` carries the
state series on the same index. `pandas.ewm(halflife=...)` covers every volatility calculation
(zero new dependencies). These two modules are the numerical foundation the allocation tilt
(Plan 03) and the tripwire (Plan 04) both consume.

Purpose: The returns-by-regime table is "the trading-crab baseline" (design §14 Phase 1) — the
historical regime-conditional stats that drive the naive tilt instead of raw point forecasts
(§7). EWMA vol feeds position sizing (∝ 1/σ̂, §6.2) and the tripwire's vol-spike signal.

Output: `assets/returns.py`, `assets/vol.py` (both in the `gap_lag.py` module shape), and two
test files seeded with the NULL-tolerance and EWMA-correctness invariants.

Note (registry / holdout, for the verifier): returns-by-regime tables are **historical
conditioning, not model selection or supervised prediction** — per CONTEXT and RESEARCH
Pitfall 4/9, NO trial-registry logging and NO purged-CV are required in v1, and reading the
full-history checkpoint (including any 2021+ rows once real data exists) is acceptable here.
This is deliberate, not an omission.
</objective>
