## Sampling Rate

- **After every task commit:** the quick run command. Include only the files that exist at that point.
- **After every plan wave:** `pytest tests/unit -q -k "platform or checkpoints or build"` plus the ratchet test.
- **Before `/gsd-verify-work`:**
  - the full suite green;
  - `ruff check src/ tests/` clean;
  - the suite green on pandas 2 (Python 3.10) as well as pandas 3.
- **Max feedback latency:** about 60 s per task.
