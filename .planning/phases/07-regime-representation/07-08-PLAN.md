<assumption_delta_decision>
**Noun now primary:** the *regime labeling pair* — classifier #1 (crisis/stress axis) plus
classifier #2 (leadership/relative axis) — replaces "the regime labeler". Wave 1 recorded
`no-change` because only one labeler existed. **This is the plan that creates the second one**,
so the question is live here in its sharpest form and is not deferrable.

**Decision: `add-alongside`** (the checkpoint's own default is `promote`; this is a reasoned
departure, not a default).

**Rationale:** D-14 locks two separate probability inputs with no product state space, which is
an add-alongside decision in all but name. Classifier #1 remains the sole L1 labeler consumed by
L2's nowcaster, by `evaluation/report.py`'s smoothed reference, and by §5.4; classifier #2 is
persisted under its own `regime_labels_2` checkpoint and enters the system only at the L4
allocation blend. Promoting #2 would re-open §5.4's ratio, the Brier and confusion tables (whose
`y_true` is classifier #1's labeling reindexed onto the decision dates), and every wave-1
measured number — and the phase's explicit non-goals leave no sanctioned remedy if that went
badly.

**Concretely, in this plan:** classifier #2 writes to new checkpoint names rather than
overwriting `regime_labels`; `labeling_2` is a new config section rather than an edit to
`labeling`; and no survivor from 07-07 is added to the `taxonomy` tiers.

**What would later force a promote:** low measured dependence (criterion 6) together with
positive joint lift on both `wealth_delta` and `dd_delta` inside the confirmed bands and a DSR
clearing 0.5; or classifier #2's own leg beating #1's on both §23.1 axes while #1's `dd_delta`
stays negative; or design freeze licensing a blend-weight sweep, at which point the fixed weight
pinned here stops being a constant and the blend earns its own ADR.
</assumption_delta_decision>
