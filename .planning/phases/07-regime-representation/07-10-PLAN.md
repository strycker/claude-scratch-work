<assumption_delta_decision>
**Noun now primary:** the *regime labeling pair* — classifier #1 (crisis/stress axis) plus
classifier #2 (leadership/relative axis) — replaces "the regime labeler".

**Decision: `add-alongside`** (the checkpoint's default is `promote`; this is reasoned, not
defaulted).

**Rationale:** D-14's "two separate probability inputs, no product state space" is an
add-alongside decision in all but name, and this is the plan where it becomes code. A product
`(state_1, state_2)` space would also thin badly: K1 times K2 cells over roughly 590 decision
months with occupancy never uniform means rare cells fall below the §4.4 five-percent floor.
Keeping two inputs blended at the weight level also makes the lift-versus-#1-alone comparison a
clean single-change ablation.

**Concretely, in this plan:** the blend function takes two probability Series and two
`returns_by_regime` tables and never forms a joint index over state pairs. Its return shape
mirrors `vol_targeted_tilt`'s exactly so nothing downstream distinguishes joint from single.

**What would later force a promote:** low measured dependence (plan 07-09) together with
positive joint lift on both axes inside the bands confirmed here and a DSR clearing 0.5; or
classifier #2's own leg beating #1's on both §23.1 axes; or design freeze licensing a
blend-weight sweep — at which point the constant pinned in ADR-0002 stops being a constant and
the blend earns its own ADR. D-14 is marked **costly** to reverse: switching to a product space
changes the allocation input contract and invalidates the lift comparison.
</assumption_delta_decision>

      the same four `vol_targeted_tilt` returns, so no downstream consumer special-cases joint
      versus single.
    - Every returned weight is greater than or equal to zero (long-only), and the weights sum to
      `scale` within `1e-12`, with `cash` equal to one minus `scale` within `1e-12`.
    - With `weight_1` equal to 1.0 the result is element-wise identical to
      `vol_targeted_tilt(probs_1, returns_by_regime_1, asset_returns, ...)` — the blend reduces
      exactly to the single-classifier path. With `weight_1` equal to 0.0 it reduces exactly to
      the classifier-#2-only path.
    - With `weight_1` equal to 0.5 and two tilts that favour different assets, the blended
      pre-scaling weight on an asset favoured only by classifier #1 equals half that asset's
      classifier-#1 weight, divided by the blended total — assertable against a hand-computed
      value.
    - The two probability inputs are never combined into a joint index: no state pair appears
      anywhere in the computation, and the two `returns_by_regime` tables are consumed
      independently.
    - Both probability inputs empty gives the degenerate result: empty weights, `cash` 1.0,
      `scale` 0.0, `portfolio_vol` NaN — matching `vol_targeted_tilt`'s own degenerate branch.
    - One probability input empty degrades to the other classifier's tilt rather than to cash;
      it does not raise.
    - A `weight_1` outside the closed unit interval raises a named `ValueError`.
