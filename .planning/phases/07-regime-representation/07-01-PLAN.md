<objective>
Freeze the L1 labeler's feature space to ONE computed-once column list, shared by the
walk-forward driver and the evaluation reference, and prove the two cannot diverge.

Purpose: audit item A13 exists because `driver.py::_window_active_features` (expanding, admits
a feature at 120 in-window months) and `report.py::_reference_label_columns` (frozen at the
first decision date) are two independent computations. §5.4 therefore compares two estimators
fit on different feature spaces. D-01 resolves this by freezing the driver — the direction that
preserves what §5.4 measures. This plan is the architectural spine of wave 1: every later
measurement in this phase is only meaningful once one call site feeds both consumers.

Output: a `frozen_l1_features` keyword threaded from a single `_reference_label_columns` call in
`report.py` through `run_backtest` into both `_refit_l1` call sites; a `trial_tag` keyword so
registry rows are attributable; and the criterion-1 equivalence test.

**Requirement coverage (read before executing).** This plan covers **REG-01 in part** — the
driver/reference feature-policy clause only. REG-01's second-classifier, orthogonality, and
joint-lift clauses are **wave 2** and are not touched here. **INV-01 is not claimed by this
plan or by any plan in this pass**: it is the invariant-candidate discovery work (M2, credit
aggregate), which `07-CONTEXT.md` **D-09** places entirely in wave 2 — *"The planner must scope
this pass to wave 1's deliverables only."* That is a deliberate, decision-backed deferral, not
an omission; it is recorded formally in the ADR written by plan 07-04.
</objective>

<assumption_delta_decision>
**Trigger:** pluralization detector fired on the term *"second"* in the phase-boundary phrase
*"add a second, independent labeler"* (`07-CONTEXT.md` domain block).

**Noun under pressure:** `regime` — today it names classifier #1's output unambiguously
(`state` column, `active_regime`, `regime_probs`, `regime_labels.yaml`). Once a second labeler
exists, a bare `regime` no longer identifies which axis is meant.

**Decision: `no-change`.**

**Rationale:** `07-CONTEXT.md` **D-09** scopes this pass to wave 1, in which exactly one labeler
exists. Renaming or promoting a generalized noun now would churn `driver.py`, `report.py`,
`allocation/tilt.py`, `allocation/hysteresis.py`, the plotting package and the persisted
artifact column names, ahead of the design that would justify it — and it would do so during
the one phase whose entire purpose is to make a set of numbers comparable across a policy
change. Stability of the naming is itself evidence quality here.

**What would force a promote:** the wave-2 planning pass, the moment classifier #2 is fit. At
that point `regime` becomes ambiguous and the primary noun should become the **labeling axis**
(e.g. a `stress` axis and a `leadership` axis, each with its own probability vector feeding
`vol_targeted_tilt` per **D-14**), with today's `regime` demoted to the stress axis's specific
name. Recorded here so the wave-2 pass inherits the decision rather than rediscovering it.
</assumption_delta_decision>
