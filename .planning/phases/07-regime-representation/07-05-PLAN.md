<objective>
Close the blocking canonicalization landmine and build classifier #2's raw feature substrate.

Purpose: `canonicalize_states` hardcodes its ordering key to `trailing_return_1m`, one of
classifier #1's 13 lean columns. D-10 requires classifier #2's raw feature set to be disjoint
from those 13, so **every** classifier-#2 fit would silently fall back to centroid column 0,
assigning arbitrary state IDs — and every occupancy, dependence and joint-lift number
downstream would then be computed against meaningless labels while criteria 5, 6 and 7 all
appeared to pass. That is the exact failure shape `UAT-AUDIT-2026-09-09` documents. Nothing in
wave 2 is trustworthy until this lands. This plan also builds the leadership/relative feature
columns and the INV-01 FRED series classifier #2 will be fit on.

Output: a keyword-only `sort_column` parameter that raises rather than warns; a new
`platform/features/relative.py` with monthly-cadence relative-strength/momentum/invariant
functions ported (never imported) from the legacy library; M2SL + TOTALSL in the monthly FRED
ingestion config; three test files covering all of it.
</objective>

<assumption_delta_decision>
**Noun now primary:** the *regime labeling pair* — classifier #1 (crisis/stress axis) plus
classifier #2 (leadership/relative axis) — replaces "the regime labeler" as the phase's
primary noun. Wave 1 recorded `no-change` because only one labeler existed; wave 2 adds the
second, so the question is live and not deferrable.

**Decision: `add-alongside`** (the checkpoint's own default is `promote`; this is a reasoned
departure, not a default).

**Rationale:** D-14 locks two separate probability inputs with no product state space.
Classifier #1 stays the sole L1 labeler consumed by L2's nowcaster, by
`evaluation/report.py`'s smoothed reference, and by §5.4. Classifier #2 enters only at the L4
allocation blend. Promoting #2 to primary would re-open §5.4's ratio, the Brier/confusion
tables (their `y_true` is the L1 labeling reindexed at `report.py:617`), and every wave-1
measured number — a cost D-06's "measurement is the gate" does not license inside this phase,
and one the explicit non-goals (no raising K on #1, no (K, λ) sweeps) leave no sanctioned
remedy for.

**What would later force a promote:** (a) criterion 6 reports low dependence AND criterion 7's
joint lift is positive on both `wealth_delta` and `dd_delta` within the confirmed bands AND the
deflated Sharpe clears 0.5 — at which point the fixed blend weight stops being a placeholder
and a promote/product design earns its own ADR; (b) classifier #2's own leg beats #1's on both
§23.1 axes while #1's `dd_delta` stays negative; (c) design freeze, when a blend-weight sweep
(forbidden now by D-13's spirit) is licensed and the blend stops being a pre-declared constant.
</assumption_delta_decision>
