<assumption_delta_decision>
**Noun now primary:** the *regime labeling pair* — classifier #1 (crisis/stress axis) plus
classifier #2 (leadership/relative axis) — replaces "the regime labeler". Wave 1 recorded
`no-change` because only one labeler existed; wave 2 built the second.

**Decision: `add-alongside`** (the checkpoint's own default is `promote`; this is a reasoned
departure, recorded rather than defaulted into).

**Rationale:** D-14 locks two separate probability inputs with no product state space, which is
an add-alongside decision in all but name. Classifier #1 remained the sole L1 labeler consumed
by L2's nowcaster, by `evaluation/report.py`'s smoothed reference and by §5.4 throughout wave 2;
classifier #2 persists to its own `regime_labels_2` checkpoint and enters only at the L4
allocation blend. Promoting it would have re-opened §5.4's ratio, the Brier and confusion tables
and every wave-1 number, with the explicit non-goals offering no sanctioned remedy if that went
badly.

**This plan is where that decision is written down permanently**, in ADR-0002's own
Consequences section, alongside the measured evidence for or against it.

**What would later force a promote:** the three conditions this phase just tested — low
dependence (07-DEPENDENCE.md), positive joint lift on both axes inside the confirmed bands
(07-JOINT-LIFT.md against 07-BANDS.md), and a DSR clearing 0.5. If all three landed, the fixed
blend weight stops being a defensible constant and a promote-or-product design earns its own
ADR; record that as a named follow-up rather than acting on it inside this phase, since D-13
forbids the blend-weight search a promote would require. If they did not all land,
`add-alongside` was the right call and ADR-0002 says so with the numbers.
</assumption_delta_decision>
