<objective>
Screen INV-01's named invariant candidates and record which survive, by name, with evidence.

Purpose: INV-01 requires four things wave 1 attempted none of — candidates constructed and
screened *using dimensional reduction as a discovery tool*, loading stability tested across
eras, every candidate logged to the trial registry and assessed walk-forward, and survivors
admitted as **named** features and never anonymous principal components (design decision R4).
Plan 07-05 ingested M2SL and TOTALSL and built the two named ratios. This plan is the screen
that decides which of them classifier #2 is allowed to be fit on, and it is the plan that keeps
R4 honest: PCA appears here only to *look at* the candidates, never to produce them.

Output: `platform/features/invariants.py`, its tests, and a screening record naming every
candidate with its verdict and the evidence behind it.
</objective>

<assumption_delta_decision>
**Noun now primary:** the *regime labeling pair* — classifier #1 (crisis/stress axis) plus
classifier #2 (leadership/relative axis) — replaces "the regime labeler". Wave 1 recorded
`no-change` because only one labeler existed; wave 2 adds the second.

**Decision: `add-alongside`** (the checkpoint's default is `promote`; this is reasoned).

**Rationale:** D-14 locks two separate probability inputs with no product state space.
Classifier #1 stays the sole L1 labeler consumed by L2, by `report.py`'s smoothed reference and
by §5.4; #2 enters only at the L4 blend. Promoting it would re-open every wave-1 number.

**Relevance to this plan specifically:** because #2 is added alongside rather than promoted,
the invariant survivors admitted here feed **only** classifier #2's feature space. They must not
be added to the `taxonomy` `fast`/`slow` tiers — doing so would widen classifier #1's lean set
past 13, breaking D-10's disjointness denominator and wave 1's criterion-1 equivalence test.

**What would later force a promote:** low measured dependence plus positive joint lift on both
axes inside the confirmed bands plus a DSR clearing 0.5; or #2's own leg beating #1's on both
§23.1 axes; or design freeze licensing a blend-weight sweep.
</assumption_delta_decision>
