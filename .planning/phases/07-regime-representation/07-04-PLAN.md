<objective>
Publish the pre/post comparison, retire the A13 caveat on the strength of its two licensing
artifacts, and record the policy decision as an ADR.

Purpose: wave 1 is not done when the numbers exist — it is done when a reader who was not here
can see what changed, why, what was rejected, and what is still open. **D-05** requires every
superseded figure to remain visible beside its replacement rather than be silently overwritten.
**D-08** removes the A13 caveat only because the cause is fixed and two concrete artifacts
license it, never because the wording softened. ROADMAP criterion 1 requires the choice and its
rejected alternatives to be an ADR.

Output: a pre/post comparison section in the backtest report, a rewritten `A13_CAVEAT`, and
`platform_design/adr/0001-l1-feature-policy.md`.

**Requirement coverage.** REG-01 **in part** — the "one documented feature policy ... recorded
as an ADR" clause. **INV-01 is not claimed and its deferral is recorded formally by this plan's
ADR** (wave 2, **D-09**).

**A13 is a policy disagreement, not a defect, and that framing must survive into the ADR.** The
driver and the report were each internally correct; §5.4 simply compared two estimators fit on
different feature spaces. The fix is a decision, which is why it earns an ADR rather than a
patch.
