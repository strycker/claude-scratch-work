<assumption_delta_decision>
**Noun now primary:** the *regime labeling pair* — classifier #1 (crisis/stress axis) plus
classifier #2 (leadership/relative axis) — replaces "the regime labeler". Wave 1 recorded
`no-change` because only one labeler existed; wave 2 adds the second, so the question is live
and not deferrable.

**Decision: `add-alongside`** (the checkpoint's own default is `promote`; this is a reasoned
departure, not a default).

**Rationale:** D-14 locks two separate probability inputs with no product state space.
Classifier #1 remains the sole L1 labeler consumed by L2's nowcaster, by `report.py`'s smoothed
reference, and by §5.4; classifier #2 enters only at the L4 allocation blend. Promoting it
would re-open §5.4's ratio, the Brier and confusion tables, and every wave-1 measured number.

**Relevance to this plan specifically:** `add-alongside` is what keeps the trial accounting
single-ledger. Because #2 is not promoted, the DSR denominator stays one cumulative count over
one registry rather than forking per labeler, and `total_trial_count()` needs no per-classifier
partitioning.

**What would later force a promote:** low measured dependence (criterion 6) together with
positive joint lift on both axes inside the confirmed bands and a DSR clearing 0.5; or
classifier #2's own leg beating #1's on both §23.1 axes; or design freeze licensing a
blend-weight sweep.
</assumption_delta_decision>
