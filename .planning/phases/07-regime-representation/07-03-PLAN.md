<objective>
Run the frozen policy for real, measure criteria 2, 3 and 4 against bands that can name the
values they reject, and execute the rejected alternative once so its rejection is evidence.

Purpose: wave 1's headline results. The freeze (07-01) and the data correction (07-02) changed
what the labeler sees; this plan measures what that did — and does so under **D-04**, where the
selection criterion is structural and pre-declared. The disagreement percentage, the §5.4 ratio
and both ablation deltas are **reported, never used to choose**. Using `dd_delta` as a veto that
sends the phase back to try another policy would be fitting the feature policy to the drawdown
metric, which is what this project's honesty framework exists to prevent.

Output: a regenerated evaluation under the frozen ten-column policy, a second logged run of the
13-feature-plus-imputation alternative, and a measurement record carrying every number with its
band and verdict.

**Requirement coverage.** REG-01 **in part** — the §5.4-interpretability and
ablation-re-measurement clauses. **INV-01 is not claimed** (wave 2, **D-09**).

**The comparison basis moved twice, and the record must say so.** 82.8% disagreement, the 0.591
§5.4 ratio, Brier 0.2087, `wealth_delta +0.379267` and `dd_delta −0.014364` were all measured
under the pre-fix expanding driver **and** the stale nine-column checkpoint. Part of any
movement is the feature-space correction, not the policy change. Attributing the whole delta to
the policy is precisely the "fooled by its own backtest" error this project exists to prevent.
Every comparison in this plan carries three labelled states: pre-fix (9-col, stale), and
post-fix (10-col) — with the plan naming which of the two causes it cannot separate.

**Cost is not a constraint.** A full 588-step L1+L2 walk-forward is ~2 minutes measured. Run
both variants for real. Do not patch persisted artifacts in place — that is the exact staleness
class that produced D-02-A.
</objective>
