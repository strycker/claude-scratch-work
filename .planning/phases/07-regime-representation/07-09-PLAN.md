<objective>
Measure whether classifier #2 actually added an axis, and record the answer honestly.

Purpose: criterion 6 is the criterion this phase can most easily fake. A dependence statistic
that is merely *returned* proves nothing; the phase needs numbers that can come out badly and be
reported as badly. ROADMAP criterion 6 states that high dependence is a **failure to add an
axis** and is recorded as such — never explained away. D-15 forbids a pre-declared threshold, so
the judgement is a human's and it is recorded. And `07-VALIDATION.md` extends this project's
third check class — "suspiciously clean" — to dependence: a perfect statistic in either
direction is a suspicion about the wiring, not a finding about the market.

Output: `platform/evaluation/dependence.py`, oracle tests that fail in both directions, the
measured dependence record, and a human judgement on it.
</objective>

**Noun now primary:** the *regime labeling pair* — classifier #1 (crisis/stress axis) plus
classifier #2 (leadership/relative axis) — replaces "the regime labeler".

**Decision: `add-alongside`** (the checkpoint's default is `promote`; this is reasoned).

**Rationale:** D-14 locks two separate probability inputs with no product state space.
Classifier #1 remains the sole L1 labeler consumed by L2, by `report.py`'s smoothed reference
and by §5.4; #2 enters only at the L4 blend. Promoting it would re-open every wave-1 number.

**Relevance to this plan specifically:** this is the plan that measures whether
`add-alongside` was worth doing at all. If dependence comes back high, the honest reading is
that the second labeler did not add an axis — which argues for neither promote nor
add-alongside but for reconsidering the feature set, a decision the explicit non-goals push to
a later phase. That outcome is recorded here in those terms, not softened into a partial
success.

**What would later force a promote:** low dependence measured here, together with positive
joint lift on both axes inside the confirmed bands and a DSR clearing 0.5; or classifier #2's
own leg beating #1's on both §23.1 axes; or design freeze licensing a blend-weight sweep.
</assumption_delta_decision>
