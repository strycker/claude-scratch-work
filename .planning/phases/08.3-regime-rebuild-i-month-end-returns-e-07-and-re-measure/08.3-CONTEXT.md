<domain>
## Phase Boundary

The honest scoreboard is re-measured on returns a trader could actually earn. Today three realized-return series
are built from monthly **average** prices:

- equities, from the multpl S&P;
- oil, from WTISPLC;
- long duration, from GS10.

Next month's return from an average includes part of a move that month-end features already saw (DECISIONS E-07).
That can flatter trend and tilt rules.

This phase:

1. Builds the **P&L** returns from **month-end** prices.
2. Re-measures all five scoreboard legs once, under a declared budget.
3. Answers two questions:
   - does the regime tilt beat the no-regime ablation?
   - does MVP-1's core mix (A-13) stand?

**Out of scope:**

- Changing features or L1 labels.
- The L2 recipe, CR-02, A-03 and REG-01, each of which is a later regime-rebuild phase.
- Implementing a new weekly allocation mode, even if D-03 triggers one. That becomes the next phase.

</domain>

<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

### Trial budget (ADR-0004)
- **Opening count:** `total_trial_count()` = **46**, read live 2026-10-02T16:55Z.
- **Budget: 2 rows**, from one `run_full_backtest_evaluation` with 2 legs (strategy and no-regime ablation) = 2 × 1.
- **Zero-row (`NO_REGISTRY`):**
  - the "before" reproduction run on HEAD;
  - the SPY, 60/40 and Faber baselines, which are registry-free arithmetic;
  - serving refits;
  - all tests.
- **Phase ceiling: 48.** The DSR hurdle at 48, with `sharpe_variance` = 1.0 (still the ADR-0002 placeholder), is
  **2.2606**.
- Overruns only by an amendment written before the run.

### Returns (what changes)
- **D-01: P&L returns only.**
  - Only the realized-return series that drive strategy and ablation P&L and the SPY / 60-40 / Faber baselines switch
    to month-end-to-month-end.
  - **Features and L1 labels stay unchanged.** A monthly average for month t is already known at t's close, so the
    features are point-in-time as they are.
  - Before and after therefore differ **only** in measured returns.
- **Month-end sources** (all free; checked reachable 2026-10-02):
  - **Equities:** `^GSPC` daily close via yfinance, 1927-12+. Month-end price, plus the existing div-yield accrual,
    with div_yield still lagged 3 per D-05 of 8.1.
  - **Long duration:** FRED `DGS10` daily, 1962-01-02+. Month-end yield, through the existing CMT par-bond
    repricing method.
  - **Oil:** FRED `DCOILWTICO` daily, 1986-01-02+.
- **D-02: Oil before 1986 keeps the monthly average, documented.**
  - Splice WTISPLC (monthly average) before 1986-01 with DCOILWTICO (month-end) from 1986-01.
  - Pre-1986 WTI was largely a posted, administered price that rarely moved within a month, so the averaging bias
    there is small.
  - Record it as a bounded exception in DECISIONS and in the E-07 caveat on the page.
- **Point-in-time:** every new raw column gets a `publication_lags` entry. Month-end market prices are lag 0. The
  completeness test enforces this.

### Re-measurement (what decides what)
- **D-03: Core-mix rule, declared before any "after" number exists.**
  - MVP-1's core switches away from the no-regime mix (A-13) **only if** a candidate (Faber 10-month SMA, 60/40 or
    SPY) beats the no-regime mix on **both**:
    - terminal log wealth **and**
    - max drawdown,

    on the common 1972–2020 window, net of 10 bps, in the same measurement.
  - If more than one candidate qualifies, the one with the higher terminal log wealth wins.
  - Otherwise the no-regime mix stays.
  - The answer is one sentence in DECISIONS.
  - If the rule triggers a switch, building that allocation mode on the weekly page is the **next** phase, not part of
    8.3.
- **D-04: Tilt vs ablation.**
  - Same rule as 8.1 (E-06): the tilt "beats" the ablation iff its terminal log wealth is greater than the ablation's,
    net of 10 bps, in the same run.
  - Drawdown and DSR (at the live count, against 2.2606) are reported alongside but do not decide.
- **D-05: Order.**
  1. A `NO_REGISTRY` "before" run on HEAD confirms the tracked 8.1 numbers reproduce exactly. If they don't, that is a
     finding: stop and report.
  2. Then the month-end change.
  3. Then the one 2-row run.
  4. Before and after are reported side by side for all five legs.
- **D-06: Data change is additive only.**
  - The tracked `monthly_raw` gains the new month-end columns.
  - Every existing column stays byte-identical, checked before saving.
  - Features, holdout and labels are not rebuilt, because D-01 leaves them unchanged.

### Page and notebooks
- **D-07: Updates.**
  - The scoreboard, P9 (N7) and the weekly page show the re-measured numbers.
  - The E-07 caveat narrows to the oil-before-1986 exception.
  - E-07 in DECISIONS is marked done.
